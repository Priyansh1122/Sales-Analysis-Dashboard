# Sales-Analysis-Dashboard

1-Project Overview- 

This project is an interactive Sales Analysis Dashboard built in Microsoft Excel using the Superstore dataset.
It provides a clear and dynamic view of sales performance across regions, categories, customers, and cities, enabling quick business insights through visual analytics.

2-Tools & Techniques Used-
Microsoft Excel,
Pivot Tables & Pivot Charts,
Slicers (Region, Category, Segment),
Timeline Filter (Order Date),
Data Cleaning & Structuring,
KPI Calculation

3-Visualizations-
Sales by Region (Bar Chart)- Compares sales across Central, East, South, and West regions,
Sales by Category (Pie Chart)- Distribution of sales among Furniture, Office Supplies, and Technology ,
Sales by Sub-Category (Donut Chart)-Detailed breakdown of product-level performance
Top 10 Customers by Sales (Bar Chart)-Identifies highest revenue-generating customers
Top 10 Cities by Sales (Line Chart)-Shows geographical sales trends

4-Key Insights Derived-
South region generates the highest sales , 
Technology category contributes the largest share
